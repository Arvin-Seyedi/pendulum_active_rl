# Experiment 07 reproduction and verification

The reviewed scientific results are in [experiment_07_report.md](experiment_07_report.md).
The [pilot report](experiment_07_pilot_report.md) and `configs/experiment_07.json`
preserve the historical pilot authorization. `configs/experiment_07_full_study.json`
authorizes the completed full study while reusing that unchanged scientific configuration.

The primary result is **0/40 PD-unsafe states with a verified safe witness** in either
execution mode. All 120 `Infeasible_Problem_Detected` statuses concern those states;
their maximum dynamics residuals range from 0.0195686 to 0.981681. Separately, all 48
converged candidates for the 16 non-equilibrium PD-safe references fail open-loop
execution safety despite small optimization residuals. Tracking retains safety on
all 16 references and improves their oracle-selected mean return by 0.053929.
Solver infeasibility and unsafe numerical execution are different observations;
neither proves physical unrecoverability. Oracle selection is not deployable
controller performance.

## Environment and tests

Use CPython 3.13.2 and `requirements_experiment_07.txt`, which includes the pinned
Experiment 06A dependencies and CasADi 3.7.2. The recorded solver build is IPOPT
3.14.11 with MUMPS 5.4.1 on macOS. The runners use POSIX process locks and process
groups; cross-platform bitwise equality is not assumed.

```sh
python3 -m venv .venv/experiment07
.venv/experiment07/bin/python -m pip install -r requirements_experiment_07.txt
.venv/experiment07/bin/python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_recoverability*.py' -v
```

The seven Experiment 07 tests use model, array, and reporting fixtures; they do not
launch optimization, simulation studies, or RL training. The source imports prior
committed helpers but no unfinished Experiment 06 IQL modules.

## Verify the existing reviewed study

In the original workspace with its complete saved artifacts and matching sources:

```sh
.venv/experiment07/bin/python experiments/07_recoverability.py --pilot --resume
.venv/experiment07/bin/python experiments/07_full_study.py --run --resume
```

With completed matching runs, these commands verify the artifact, configuration,
source, and preservation hashes and exit without new solves or rollouts. They can
resume incomplete runs, so use them for verification only when the completion
markers already exist. Retain the historical preservation snapshots and source
hashes; do not rewrite them to accept changed files. The full study also retains
399 evaluated trajectories and bitwise recorded-action replays, all 201 raw states
per trajectory, and the twelve paired pilot solver checks.

## Fresh reproduction in a disposable checkout

Generated data and logs are intentionally excluded from Git. Experiment 07 needs
the completed Experiment 06A `state_action_manifest.npz` and
`PD_reference_replay.npz` under `results/offline_rl/experiment_06A/`; it needs no D0,
acquired training datasets, checkpoints, or IQL files. These two inputs can be
restored from the reviewed archive or regenerated by the committed Experiment 06A
runner. The full Experiment 07 runner additionally requires its completed pilot.

Use a **separate disposable checkout**, not the reviewed workspace. Before starting
any reproduction, move its copies of the two reviewed Experiment 07 reports aside:

```sh
mkdir reviewed_reports
mv docs/experiment_07_pilot_report.md docs/experiment_07_report.md reviewed_reports/
```

This is necessary because the preserved runners write generated reports to both
`results/` and `docs/` and refuse to overwrite a differing reviewed report. Timing
and protected-file counts necessarily vary between checkouts. Moving only these
copies before snapshots are created lets the unchanged runners finish while
retaining the reviewed documents. Do not remove or move reports in the original
reviewed workspace.

After installing the dependencies, use the fixed configurations:

```sh
# Only if the two completed 06A prerequisite artifacts have not been restored:
.venv/experiment07/bin/python experiments/06a_counterfactual_actions.py --run
.venv/experiment07/bin/python experiments/07_recoverability.py --pilot
.venv/experiment07/bin/python experiments/07_full_study.py --run
```

These are reproduction instructions, not additional scientific observations.
The full runner reuses the four pilot cases and executes the other 53 states;
it retains all three fixed starts and both execution modes without tuning.
Existing result directories require explicit `--resume`. Interrupted solver logs
without a completed attempt record are preserved and require inspection; the
runner does not silently replace them.

Results remain under `results/recoverability/pilot/` and
`results/recoverability/full_study/`. Keep trajectories, solver logs, plots,
discrepancy arrays, timing records, and manifests outside Git. Compare scientific
outcomes and trajectory/replay checks separately from runtime costs, absolute
paths, repository metadata, and preservation counts.
